About Us

A packaging machinery manufacturer, exporter and turnkey solution provider, founded in 2014.

Our story

Dishita Automation is an engineering house trusted in packaging and special purpose machinery. We use CNC machining and world-class instruments to build our machines.

Mr. Anthony Vinson left a comfortable career to start the company as a proprietorship. It began with the design, manufacture and marketing of automatic rinsing, filling and sealing machines for cups, and bottle rinsing, filling and capping machines for water, juice, jam and chemicals. Today the range also covers BOPP labeling machines, shrink sleeve applicators, shrink tunnels and conveyors.

Our machines are built in quality stainless steel and aluminium, or to the specification you need. We have a strong reputation among major customers in India.

Quality control

Every machine is inspected, tested exhaustively and soak tested for many days before shipment. We build to industry norms with high grade raw material and current technology.

Our team

Our R&D and production team has over 10 years of combined experience. They modify an existing machine or design a new one to your exact requirement.

Technology

We balance process and product technology, choose sensors to fit your application, and configure special sample conditioners to fit the system to your process.
